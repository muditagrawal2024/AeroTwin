Fly a drone three ways, right in the browser.

AeroTwin is a quadcopter simulator with real flight physics. Fly it with your keyboard, plug in a gamepad, or snap blocks together into an autonomous mission. Every flight lands in your logbook, ready to replay in 3D.

Keyboard

The two hands mirror the two sticks of a real transmitter: W A S D for throttle and yaw, the arrow keys for pitch and roll. Keys ease in, so a short tap gives a gentle nudge.

Gamepad

Any Xbox, PlayStation or generic controller works the moment you press a button. Mode 2 by default, Mode 1 on request, with adjustable dead zone and expo for precise hovering.

Block missions

Write autonomous flights with Blockly: loops, variables, functions and sensors, plus orbit, curve and go-to moves. Grab the sticks during a mission and you are back in control.

A small district to explore

  • Vertiport with a lit landing pad, your take-off and return-to-home point.
  • Ring course of eight floating gates over the lake, timed per lap.
  • Radio tower for point-of-interest orbits with the camera locked on.
  • City blocks with a rooftop pad for precision landings.
  • Day, dusk and night, wind gusts, sport mode and a draining battery.

Every flight is kept

  • Telemetry recorded ten times a second from take-off to landing.
  • Ghost replays of any past flight, with scrubbing and speed control.
  • Photos and video from the stabilised gimbal camera.
  • Missions saved with their earlier versions, and Drone Commander programs import as they are.
